Auxiliaries matter

Rated kW × average electrical power fraction × annual hours = kWh. B’s fans use 350 × 0.48 × 3,600 = 604,800 kWh/year. A power fraction is not a speed fraction.

01Existing condition & future capitalHIGHEST PRIORITY

A’s assumed $800,000 Year 3 and $600,000 Year 7 work makes avoided expenditure a major benefit. The validation report’s severe diagnostic—removing only those baseline events and related depreciation—takes C’s NPV to −$950,575. Obtain service records and a condition-based capital plan.

02Installed cost & scopePLANNING ESTIMATE

Both budgets reconcile, including 10% contingency on real-property items. Neither has a contractor quotation. Validate quantities, rigging, shutdowns, temporary cooling and BAS/workstation overlap. C’s break-even funding is $1,741,161, only $72,161 above base.

03Tariff & ownership of savingsOWNER ASSUMPTION

The $0.20/kWh avoided tariff and 100% owner retention are assumptions. Collect 12–24 months of bills, interval demand and lease clauses. The report’s 50% retention diagnostic reduces C’s NPV to −$426,932. Do not double-count demand savings.

04Delivered energy & maintenance savingsUNMEASURED INPUTS

Auxiliaries provide 75.5% of C’s modeled electricity savings. Measure fan/pump power and chiller performance, calibrate operating schedules, and verify minimum flow and ventilation. At 70% energy realization, C NPV is −$80,421; at 60% maintenance realization it is −$160,465, each tested separately.

05Tax, horizon & emissions boundarySCOPE LIMITATIONS

The model uses an illustrative 28% tax rate, immediate deduction use, 39-year real-property depreciation and five-year computer MACRS; no bonus or Section 179 deduction is assumed. Classification and eligibility need owner-specific review.

The ten-year horizon excludes terminal service value and later tax benefits, particularly relevant to B. Debt, incentives and avoided carbon penalties are excluded. The LL97 screen leaves every alternative above the modeled 2030 limit and omits 2035–2036; it is not a compliance determination. Gas consumption is unchanged.
